Plain-language summary: Aivora helps students learn and plan careers. Accounts and saved progress use Supabase; optional AI tools use Google Gemini. Don't submit sensitive information to AI tools or publicly shared portfolios.

01 / What Aivora is

Aivora AI is a student-built educational and career-guidance prototype. It provides career exploration, skill self-assessments, learning check-ins, project tracking, portfolio tools and an AI career mentor. This notice describes the personal information used when you access Aivora through this website.

02 / Information we receive

  • Account information: your email address, and the name you provide. If you choose Google sign-in, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name, email address and profile picture through Supabase authentication.
  • Saved student activity: your selected career pathway, skill ratings, learning progress, quiz and practice results, projects, milestones, saved job applications, portfolio details and other information you enter into your workspace.
  • Information you submit to tools: public GitHub usernames or links, optional portfolio contact details, and prompts sent to the AI Mentor. If you choose AI resume review, your pasted resume text is sent to the AI service for that specific review.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication providers may process basic connection information, session details and logs needed to keep the service working and secure.

03 / Google account sign-in

If you use “Continue with Google,” Aivora requests only the basic information needed for authentication (such as identity and email). Google handles your Google password; Aivora does not receive your Google password. Aivora does not request permission to read your Gmail messages, Google Drive files or Google contacts.

Your Google sign-in information is used to establish your Supabase session, identify your account and connect it with your saved student progress. Using Google sign-in is optional when email/password sign-in is available.

04 / How your information is used

We use account and student activity information to let you sign in, restore progress, personalize career-related tools, respond to your questions, provide requested features, troubleshoot problems and protect the platform. Career scores are educational indicators based on the information supplied; they are not hiring decisions or formal qualifications.

05 / AI Mentor and resume reviews

Aivora uses Google Gemini for certain optional AI features. When you submit an AI Mentor question or explicitly request AI resume feedback, the prompt and relevant context are sent through Aivora’s backend to Google’s AI service to produce a response. Google may process that content under its applicable service terms and privacy practices. Do not submit passwords, government IDs, financial details, private addresses or other sensitive information in prompts or resumes.

The local resume checklist is designed to check text in your browser; using that checklist alone does not require an AI request. AI-generated guidance can be incorrect and should be checked independently.

06 / Where information is stored and who processes it

Supabase provides user authentication and cloud storage for account-linked progress. Your browser may also keep account-associated progress in local storage to support the interface. Vercel hosts the website and runs its API functions. Google provides sign-in and Gemini AI processing when those features are used. Public GitHub information may be retrieved from GitHub when you choose to analyze a public profile or repository.

These service providers process information under their own applicable policies and contracts. Aivora does not request access to private GitHub repositories.

07 / Sharing and public portfolio downloads

We do not intentionally sell student profile information to advertisers. Information is shared with the technical providers described above as needed to operate the requested features, or if legally required. Your personal dashboard is not automatically published as a public portfolio.

If you download and publish a portfolio website yourself, anything included in that exported page (such as your name, project links or chosen contact email) may become public where you choose to host it. Review those details before sharing.

08 / Data controls, deletion and retention

You can edit information in your student workspace and reset certain saved career progress using the application controls. If you want help with account deletion, access or corrections, contact ception@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. We will review your request and explain what can be deleted or must be retained for security or legal reasons.

We retain account-linked information while the account and related records remain in the system, subject to operational needs and requests. Third-party services may maintain their own technical logs and retention periods.

09 / Security and age-related guidance

We use established hosting and authentication services and access controls to help protect accounts. No online service can promise absolute security. Use a strong password if you choose email/password sign-in. If you are below the age required to consent to online services where you live, use Aivora with the necessary parent or guardian permission.

10 / Updates and contact

We may update this notice as Aivora changes, including when new tools or data uses are introduced. The current date appears at the top of this page. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email ception@gmail.com.
