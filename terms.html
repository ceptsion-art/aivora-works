Plain-language summary: Aivora helps students learn and plan careers. Accounts and saved progress use Supabase; optional AI tools use Google Gemini. Don't submit sensitive information to AI tools or publicly shared portfolios.

01 / About the service

Aivora AI is a student-built educational prototype offering career exploration, study guidance, projects, learning activities, portfolio creation and optional AI-powered mentoring. It is provided for personal learning and demonstration, not as a recruitment, admission, employment or professional certification service.

02 / Accounts and access

You may use a supported email/password account or Google sign-in. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your credentials and activity on your account. Do not attempt to access another student’s data or interfere with the platform. You may request assistance with your account by contacting the address below.

03 / Acceptable use

  • Use Aivora lawfully and respectfully.
  • Do not enter, publish or upload content you do not have rights to use.
  • Do not abuse login, AI or public GitHub lookup features through automated or excessive requests.
  • Do not submit secrets, passwords or other sensitive personal information to AI tools or public portfolio exports.
  • Do not attempt to bypass security controls or access private data.

04 / Educational and AI limitations

Learning scores, career recommendations, interview feedback, roadmaps and resume checks are informational and may be incomplete or incorrect. They are not formal evaluations or guarantees of admission, job offers, salary, placement or qualifications.

Google Gemini may generate inaccurate or unsuitable text. Verify technical claims and important career decisions using trusted sources and qualified people. Aivora may refuse requests outside its education and career-help scope.

05 / Your content and portfolio exports

You remain responsible for the information and projects you submit. By submitting material to a feature, you authorize the processing reasonably necessary to provide that feature. If you export and publicly host a portfolio, you—not Aivora—choose what information becomes public and where it is published.

06 / Third-party services and links

Aivora relies on Supabase for account services, Vercel for hosting, Google for sign-in and certain AI features, and may access public GitHub data. Third-party services and external links are governed by their own policies and terms. Their availability and usage limits may change.

07 / Availability and changes

This is an evolving college prototype. Features may change, experience downtime, hit free-tier quotas or be discontinued without advance notice. While we try to keep the service useful and stable, continuous availability is not guaranteed. Accounts may be limited if they are used abusively or pose security risks.

08 / Fees

At the time of this notice, Aivora is offered as a free student demonstration and does not collect payments through the website. If paid features are ever introduced, their terms will need to be explained separately before charging users.

09 / Disclaimer and responsibility

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the service is offered on an “as available” basis without a guarantee that every result will be accurate, uninterrupted or suitable for a particular purpose. Nothing in these terms removes rights that applicable consumer or privacy laws do not allow us to exclude.

10 / Privacy, updates and contact

Our Privacy Policy describes how student information is processed. We may revise these terms as the prototype evolves and will update the date shown above. For service questions or account requests, contact ception@gmail.com.
